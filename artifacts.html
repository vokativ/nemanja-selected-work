Challenge

Family logistics arrive as fragmented, noisy inputs — WhatsApp messages, school PDFs, paper forms, voice notes, and work calendar photos — creating high mental overhead and missed preparation steps across the family unit.

Enterprise trust Copy link

Challenge

Production analytics and AI touched company, customer, billing, and athlete data — but the inherited setup had fragile VM deployments, stale access, scattered keys, little disaster recovery, and overly broad permissions.

The Premise Lab

A public, open-source example of using AI to think before building: surfacing hidden premises, testing real-world assumptions, and turning rough ideas into sharper artifacts.

Public open source Copy link
Open source + Gemini + Opal AI for judgment, not just code Philosophy as a build tool
Challenge

AI is getting good at building — which lets weak ideas harden faster. The expensive failure comes earlier: unclear premises, vague words, and hidden beliefs about how the world works.

What I did

Built a public, open-source thinking partner combining philosophy, behavioral science, and systems thinking, so you can examine an idea before building it. It asks what must be true, what would prove the idea wrong, and which words carry more confusion than meaning — without making you perform intelligence for the machine.

Impact

A rough thought becomes a working hypothesis, a clearer brief, a reality test, and a ledger of assumptions still owed to the world — AI used to improve judgment before action.

GitHub repo Gemini Gem Opal app with Google Docs output

Idea stress-test flow

Mirror: restate the idea in higher resolution before judging it.
X-ray: surface the load-bearing assumption or category mistake.
Reframe: turn the idea into a sharper working hypothesis.
Contact: propose the smallest useful reality test before building.

hocudaglasam.com

A public civic-tech product that simplified Serbian diaspora voter registration while keeping sensitive personal data on the user's device.

Public project Copy link
Several thousand registered Reused across election cycles On-device PII, open source
Challenge

Serbian citizens abroad faced a clumsy registration: find the right embassy, download and print forms, fill them by hand, scan, and email everything to the correct address.

What I did

Built an open-source Angular/TypeScript app that generated the registration document client-side and routed it to the right embassy. The core decision was privacy by architecture — no server-side PII; sensitive data stayed on the user's device, and the public code let citizens and institutions inspect the behavior.

Impact

Several thousand registered, the tool was reused in later cycles, and it drew institutional scrutiny and press — making transparency and clear privacy communication as important as the workflow itself.
